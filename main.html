Manali


Manali is a beautiful hill station located in Himachal Pradesh, India. It is famous for its snow-covered mountains, scenic valleys, adventure sports, and peaceful environment.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Solang Valley - Famous for adventure activities like paragliding and skiing.
  2. Rohtang Pass - A stunning mountain pass with breathtaking views.
  3. Hadimba Temple - An ancient temple surrounded by cedar forests.
  4. Old Manali - Known for its cozy cafes and peaceful atmosphere.
  5. Jogini Waterfalls - A beautiful waterfall perfect for trekking lovers.

Must-Try Foods

  • Siddu - A traditional Himachali bread dish.
  • Thukpa - A delicious Tibetan noodle soup.
  • Babru - A Himachali version of kachori.
  • Trout Fish - Fresh and flavorful local fish dish.
  • Mittha - A sweet Himachali dessert made with rice and dry fruits.
